About Us

CySecuLab

CySecuLab builds security technology that protects the trust boundaries between people, between people and AI, and between AI and AI. Every product grows from hardware-rooted trust technology, from the hardware execution layer to networks and AI inputs.

Products

Every product grows from hardware-rooted trust technology

T-MAF

Trusted Multi Agent Framework
Protects personal data, enterprise data and AI models end to end, and keeps multi-agent interaction monitored under security policy.

DPF

Dialect Perimeter Filter
Protocol-dialect network trust control: protocol dialects admit only authorized connections. Core technology born from research funded by the U.S. Office of Naval Research.

CC-BOX

Confidential Computing Platform
Protects code and data during computation.

CC-SBOM

Confidential Computing SBOM
Performs SBOM extraction, management and sharing safely on confidential computing, while keeping the original program protected.

Outerstellar

HW Instruction-flow Monitor
A hardware security monitor that watches instruction flow beside the CPU core. It extends Interstellar (ACM CCS 2024) to high-performance processors: security without slowdown.
